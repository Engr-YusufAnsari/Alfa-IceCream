Our Story

Uncompromising Quality

Alfa Kulfi & Ice Cream has been serving authentic Indian frozen desserts in Mumbai for years, crafting every kulfi, falooda and ice cream in-house using traditional recipes, premium ingredients. From timeless favourites like Malai, Kesar Pista and Punjabi Kulfi to seasonal specials, every treat is made fresh daily for a rich, authentic taste.
